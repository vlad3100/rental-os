# Interview Script (English) — v0, 2026-11-08

> **Authorship:** drafted by Claude (AI assistant) at the founder's request. Read it aloud once, cut it down and put it into your own words before the first interview.
> Question numbers match `interview-script-de.md` so notes stay comparable. Use English for international agents, relocation contacts and English-speaking landlords.

## For the interviewer (do not read out)

- **Goal:** facts about the **last real letting** — not opinions about our idea. No pitch. No prototype.
- **Write down numbers** with their source: estimate / records / observed.
- Probe: "And then?", "How did you notice?", "Can you give me an example?" Let silence work.
- **No leading questions** ("Wouldn't it be great if…?").
- **Never record personal data of applicants or owners.** Counts, categories and durations only.
- **Record audio only with explicit consent.** Secret recording is a criminal offence in Germany (§ 201 StGB).
- If the interviewee describes a legally problematic practice (e.g. selecting by origin): do not lecture, do not dig deeper, note it as a risk — **never turn it into a product requirement**.
- Length: 30–40 minutes. Always ask for a referral at the end.

## 0. Opening and consent (2 min)

> "Thank you for your time. My name is Vlad Alekseev. I'm building a tool for rental professionals, and before building anything I want to understand how inquiries and viewings really work in practice. I'm not presenting or selling anything today.
> This takes about 30 minutes. May I take notes? *[Optional:]* May I record the call? I'll use the recording only for my own analysis and delete it within 30 days.
> Please don't mention names or contact details of applicants or owners. Feel free to skip any question."

## 1. Context (5 min)

1. Tell me briefly about your office: how many people work there, and who handles lettings?
2. How many flats did you let in the last twelve months? How much of your work is letting compared with sales or management?
3. What kind of flats are they usually? (size, location, furnished or unfurnished, open-ended or fixed-term)
4. *(Optional)* How has the letting business changed for you since the Bestellerprinzip (the party who orders the agent pays)?

## 2. The last letting (15 min) — core

5. Think of the last flat you let. When did the listing go live, and when was the lease signed?
6. Where did you advertise? Roughly how many inquiries came in — on day one and in total? How do you know the number?
7. What did you do with the inquiries, step by step? Who did each step?
8. Did you pause or deactivate the listing at some point? When, and why?
9. How do you decide who gets an invitation to a viewing? *(Ask about the process only; do not judge.)*
10. Which questions did people ask again and again? Can you name the top three?
11. Which information or documents do you ask for at which point — before the viewing, after the viewing, right before the contract? What do you do with documents that arrive unasked?
12. How did you organise the viewings — individual slots or one group viewing? How many viewings were there, and how many people didn't show up?
13. How much time did all of this take — from the first inquiry to the last viewing? How is that split between reading, replying and scheduling? How sure are you about that number?
14. What went wrong or took longer than necessary? Did an inquiry get lost or answered too late? How did you notice?
15. Who made the final decision on who got the flat?

## 3. Tools and spend (5 min)

16. Which tools do you use for this? (portal inbox, CRM such as onOffice, Propstack or FlowFact, email, Excel, calendar, text templates)
17. What runs automatically — auto-replies, automatic exposé dispatch, online viewing booking?
18. Roughly what do you pay for that per month? Who chose the tool?
19. Have you ever tried to improve or hand off this part of the work — to an assistant, a working student, software or AI? What happened?

## 4. Boundaries and risks (3 min)

20. What would you never leave to software? Why not?
21. What worries you legally or about data protection when handling inquiries and applicant data?
22. Do you use AI tools like ChatGPT at work? For what — and explicitly not for what?

## 5. Value and next steps (5 min)

23. Suppose this part of the work took half as long on your next flat: what would you do with the time you save?
24. What would that time saving be worth to you per letting? *(Never suggest a price. Ask only after question 13 has been answered.)*
25. If you let a new flat in the next few weeks: would you be open to me shadowing the process once and measuring the time? The data stays in your system, and you approve every message yourself.
26. Who else should I talk to? Would you introduce me?

> **Close:** "Thank you! If you like, I'll send you an anonymised summary of what I learn across all conversations."

## Variants

### Segment C — professional private landlords (2–50 units)
Replace questions 1–4 with:
- C1. How many flats do you let, and how often do tenants change per year?
- C2. Do you handle lettings yourself or sometimes hire an agent? When and why?
- C3. How many hours per month do you spend on letting tasks?
Then continue from question 5. For question 15 they usually decide themselves — record the process only.

### Adjacent interviewees (property manager, CRM consultant, relocation agency)
- X1. How often do you handle new lettings, and who does the work?
- X2. Which parts of the letting process cause the most questions or complaints from your clients or team?
- X3. Which tools do you see in the market for this, and what do they lack?
- X4. Where do automation or AI fail in this area today?
- X5. Which interfaces or exports do the common systems really offer?
- X6. Who should I definitely talk to?

### Applicants (optional, secondary)
- T1. Tell me about your last flat search in Munich: how many inquiries did you send, and how many replies came back?
- T2. Which replies were helpful and which were not?
- T3. Which documents were you asked for, and at which point?
*(No questions about origin, family, health or other sensitive characteristics.)*
